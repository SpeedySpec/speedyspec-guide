## Behaviour

Native: `post.php`'s `sanitize_post_field()` is a line of PHP over `SpeedySpec\WpPosts\Native\sanitize_post_field`, which dispatches the `SanitizePostField` message to the handler bound at `SpeedySpec\WpPosts\SanitizePostFieldHandler`, so a handler PHP binds there takes the call. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-posts-php/src/posts.php#L20-L23" hash="fdf2713b6939208cfe3326d2f3f534ff6f3f1149b81337301f2b0872f6177998"/> <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-posts/src/sanitize.rs#L107-L121" hash="b10925b6e51a9de38e7bea917e67a591ef914e184c533e132973fa9914c82a4b"/>

It ports WordPress's branches in order: the int fields through `(int)`, `raw` as it is, then for `edit` the `edit_{$field}` and `{$field_no_prefix}_edit_pre` filters and `format_to_edit()`, for `db` `pre_{$field}` and `{$field_no_prefix}_save_pre`, and for display contexts `{$field}`, `esc_attr()` for `attribute` and `esc_js()` for `js`. Any other context word is treated as `display`.
