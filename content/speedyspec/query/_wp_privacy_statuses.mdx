## Behaviour

Served by WordPress's PHP; the Rust rules below are not wired yet. The runtime worker links neither posts crate, so PHP on SpeedySpec runs WordPress's own `post.php` until phase 4 of the posts and meta port. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-php-runtime/Cargo.toml#L20-L55" hash="955f1ccec24cc11da3fa0826db96868561c6e6c3cdc55277b37b80c09b513508"/>

## The Rust rule

`privacy_statuses` gives the four `request-*` statuses' labels, in the `request status` context, each label through the `Translator` port. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-posts/src/status.rs#L167-L176" hash="de0e422bee4bfe527ee8098ee25d48b0aa1d1ce10b010679ec232a1764d8cfa9"/>

The recorded table matches WordPress 7.1.5's, in order. <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-posts::recorded::status_label_tables_match_wordpress"/>
