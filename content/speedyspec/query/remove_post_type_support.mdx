## Behaviour

Served by WordPress's PHP; the Rust rules below are not wired yet. The runtime worker links neither posts crate, so PHP on SpeedySpec runs WordPress's own `post.php` until phase 4 of the posts and meta port. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-php-runtime/Cargo.toml#L24-L60" hash="4e45d1d171d753b4dfe1c47e78d7b37982c3572c9fc8cf407854fed9c53b58e9"/>

## The Rust rule

`PostTypeRegistry::remove_support` unsets one feature. The type's entry stays, empty, after its last feature goes, as in WordPress. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-posts/src/registry.rs#L338-L339" hash="c0318824e311ad8c2181f110efa4ac3adb32c8c1df4a576a5f7dc30bd3a22d1a"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-posts::registry::remove_last_feature_keeps_the_type_entry"/>

The recorded `supports-api` scenario replays as in WordPress 7.2-RC2. <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-posts::recorded::post_type_scenarios_match_wordpress"/>
