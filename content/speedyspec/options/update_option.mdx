Native: the `UpdateOption` command. Every recorded scenario replays as in WordPress, including an unchanged value, a missing option (which is added) and a serialized value equal to the stored one. <Ev scenario="update_option_unchanged" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="update_option_missing_adds" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="update_option_serialized_equal" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="protected_update_option_alloptions" area="options"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-php::wordpress::wordpress_option_runs_pass"/>
