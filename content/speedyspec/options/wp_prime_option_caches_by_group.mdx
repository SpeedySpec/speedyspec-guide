## Behaviour

Native: the `PrimeOptionCachesByGroup` command. It reads the group from `$new_allowed_options` through `SpeedySpec\WpOptions\allowed_options()`. <Ev scenario="prime_by_group" area="options"/>

## Override

Options live in `wp_options` behind the `OptionRepository` port; the use case is a handler in the container. See the architecture page.
