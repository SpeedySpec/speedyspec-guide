## Behaviour

Native: the `GetRegisteredSettings` query, read from `$wp_registered_settings`. <Ev scenario="register_setting_defaults" area="options"/>

## Differences

Entries lack WordPress 7.2's `show_in_abilities`; see [register_setting](/speedyspec/options/register_setting/).

## Override

The registry's state lives behind the `SettingsStore` port; in PHP it is `$wp_registered_settings` and `$new_allowed_options`. See the architecture page.
