## Behaviour

Native: the `GetOption` query, answered in Rust from the `alloptions` and `options` caches and `wp_options`. Every recorded scenario of `get_option()` replays as in WordPress 7.2-RC2: the hook order, the `notoptions` cache, short-circuits, odd names and every kind of stored value. <Ev scenario="get_from_alloptions" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="get_from_database" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="get_missing_notoptions" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="pre_option_short_circuits" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="value_stdclass" area="options"/> WordPress's own option tests pass against it. <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-php::wordpress::wordpress_option_runs_pass"/>

A read with no PHP callback on its hooks never enters PHP. <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-php::php::no_crossing_without_php_callbacks"/>

`siteurl`, `home`, `category_base`, `tag_base` and, as in WordPress 7.2, `wp_random_base` lose their trailing slash. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/option.php#L239-L241" hash="5a00413326e716a198c19a36084b2ac036b5a59dc64c79dcd245f0bf667894bb"/> <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-options/src/read.rs#L92-L102" hash="17d2410a38d17302956ff2cb875365dd3cf714658820f5b68a2b473b9ab63da7"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-tests::read::random_base_is_untrailingslashed"/>
