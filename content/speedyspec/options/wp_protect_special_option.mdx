## Behaviour

Native: the `ProtectSpecialOption` command. For `alloptions` and `notoptions` it calls WordPress's `wp_die()` with WordPress's message, through the `ProtectedOptions` port; if `wp_die()` returns, the write still doesn't happen. <Ev scenario="protect_special_option_direct" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="protected_update_option_notoptions" area="options"/> <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-options-php/src/adapters.rs#L189-L220" hash="def7ad779da3b8e19fb9878b520943a6f45796623be2bd05d186b1bd89043059"/>
