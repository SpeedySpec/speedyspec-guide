## Behaviour

Native: the `RegisterSetting` command, handled by `SettingsRegistry`. Defaults, a callable as `$args`, the deprecated groups and the `register_setting_args` filter replay as in WordPress 7.1.5. <Ev scenario="register_setting_defaults" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="register_setting_callable" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="register_setting_deprecated_groups" area="options"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-php::wordpress::wordpress_option_runs_pass"/>

## Differences

WordPress 7.2 adds `show_in_abilities` (default `false`) to every registered setting; SpeedySpec's defaults don't have it, so `get_registered_settings()` entries lack the key (found by the guide). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/option.php#L3081-L3089" hash="cbc6c79cadd1f6e48dc49a0efb9ce7f6faaf7481c70029a1d011470a43e8fa5d"/> <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-options/src/settings.rs#L182-L189" hash="37cd3eafcc5f32c23b2029cbe28f8599733ef0ef198177668f778ef212bad534"/>

## Override

The registry's state lives behind the `SettingsStore` port; in PHP it is `$wp_registered_settings` and `$new_allowed_options`. See the architecture page.
