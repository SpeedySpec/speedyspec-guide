## Behaviour

Native: the `RegisterSetting` command, handled by `SettingsRegistry`. Defaults, a callable as `$args`, the deprecated groups and the `register_setting_args` filter replay as in WordPress 7.2-RC2. <Ev scenario="register_setting_defaults" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="register_setting_callable" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="register_setting_deprecated_groups" area="options"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-php::wordpress::wordpress_option_runs_pass"/>

The defaults are 7.2's, `show_in_abilities => false` after `show_in_rest`, so every entry of `get_registered_settings()` has the key, as the recording shows. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/option.php#L3081-L3089" hash="cbc6c79cadd1f6e48dc49a0efb9ce7f6faaf7481c70029a1d011470a43e8fa5d"/> <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-options/src/settings.rs#L182-L190" hash="aefa853a2ae4a855e7ffe0517fced13c15c2df67a5658b2dc951fd0934d4fa95"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-memory::settings::defaults_merged_in_order"/>

## Override

The registry's state lives behind the `SettingsStore` port; in PHP it is `$wp_registered_settings` and `$new_allowed_options`. See the architecture page.
