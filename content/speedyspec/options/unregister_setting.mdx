## Behaviour

Native: the `UnregisterSetting` command. It removes the setting's sanitize and default filters and fires `unregister_setting`. <Ev scenario="unregister_setting_removes_default" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="register_setting_deprecated_groups" area="options"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-memory::settings::unregister_removes_filters_and_fires"/>

## Override

The registry's state lives behind the `SettingsStore` port; in PHP it is `$wp_registered_settings` and `$new_allowed_options`. See the architecture page.
