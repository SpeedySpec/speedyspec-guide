## Behaviour

Native: the `DetermineOptionAutoloadValue` query, decided by the `AutoloadPolicy` port (WordPress's rules in `WordPressAutoloadPolicy`). Every autoload value a caller can pass replays as in WordPress. <Ev scenario="determine_autoload_value_direct" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="autoload_requested_weird" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="autoload_requested_none" area="options"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-options-php::wordpress::wordpress_option_runs_pass"/>

## Override

Bind another `AutoloadPolicy` from Rust, or hook `wp_default_autoload_value` as in WordPress. See the architecture page.
