**Differences.** The port follows WordPress 7.1.5, and this guide documents 7.2, which changed three ported functions: `get_option()` also untrailingslashes `wp_random_base`, `wp_load_core_site_options()` no longer primes `can_compress_scripts`, and `register_setting()` adds `show_in_abilities` to every setting. SpeedySpec has none of these yet. <Ev source="ss:compat/options/source.json#L2" hash="207fbaef291a95e22bbc8c4c534640238e0ee50a876206e789d09dfdc0e9443b"/> SpeedySpec also defines `$new_allowed_options` and `$new_whitelist_options` at the start of every request, where WordPress waits for the first `register_setting()`. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-options-php/src/options.php#L221-L226" hash="ab61c6201b01f0001e7b6696b1cb1c34dee91f333b8196f492e8d6b8bde8940c"/> Each is on its page.
