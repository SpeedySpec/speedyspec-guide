## Behaviour

Declared by SpeedySpec's `cron.php` with WordPress's body: registers `_wp_cron()` on `shutdown`, runs it at once during `shutdown`, or with `ALTERNATE_WP_CRON` on `wp_loaded`. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-cron-php/src/cron.php#L347-L368" hash="9e6b429182f70b50dc3720c86132b43a0aff544f7eb78ff5b4a2c3d1215adb7a"/> WordPress's two `wp_cron()` tests pass with it. <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-cron-php::wordpress::wordpress_cron_tests_pass"/> No compat scenario records it.

What `_wp_cron()` then does differs; see [spawn_cron](/speedyspec/cron/spawn_cron/).
