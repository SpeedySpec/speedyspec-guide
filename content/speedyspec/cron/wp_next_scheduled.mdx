## Behaviour

Native. Returns the timestamp of the next event for a hook and arguments, through `wp_next_scheduled`. Every recorded scenario replays as in WordPress, and WordPress's own cron tests pass. <Ev area="cron" scenario="single_event_args"/> <Ev area="cron" scenario="numeric_hook_and_string_timestamp"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-cron-php::wordpress::wordpress_cron_tests_pass"/>

## Override

The `cron` option is read and written through the `CronStore` port. To keep events somewhere else, build `CronApi` with another store; see the architecture page.
