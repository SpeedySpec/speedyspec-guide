## Behaviour

Native. Returns the events due now, with `pre_get_ready_cron_jobs`. Every recorded scenario replays as in WordPress, and WordPress's own cron tests pass. <Ev area="cron" scenario="ready_jobs"/> <Ev area="cron" scenario="ready_jobs_filtered"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-cron-php::wordpress::wordpress_cron_tests_pass"/>

## Override

The `cron` option is read and written through the `CronStore` port. To keep events somewhere else, build `CronApi` with another store; see the architecture page.
