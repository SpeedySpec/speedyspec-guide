## Behaviour

The PHP side of the hook store port: a PHP object can be bound as the store, and every registration then goes to it instead of the in-memory store. <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-hooks-php::php_store::php_hook_store_binding_takes_every_registration"/>

## Override

Bind your own store under the container's hook-store key; see the architecture page.
