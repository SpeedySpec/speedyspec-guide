## Behaviour

`$wp_filter` is a proxy (`SpeedySpec\WpHooks\HookGlobal`) over the native hook store. Reading it, indexing it, iterating it, unsetting a hook and setting a new `WP_Hook` behave as in WordPress. <Ev scenario="wp_filter_read_and_index"/> <Ev scenario="wp_filter_iterate"/> <Ev scenario="wp_filter_unset_hook"/> <Ev scenario="wp_filter_set_new_hook_object"/>

## Porting

- Don't replace `$wp_filter` to clear hooks; remove the callbacks of the hooks you own with `remove_all_filters()`. <Ev break="wp_filter_replace_wholesale"/>
- Don't put plain arrays at a hook name after WordPress has loaded; set a `WP_Hook`, or use `add_filter()`. <Ev break="wp_filter_set_plain_array"/>
- To register hooks before WordPress loads, leave arrays in `$wp_filter` as WordPress documents; SpeedySpec imports them. Don't build `WP_Hook` objects and replace the global with them. <Ev break="wp_hook_build_preinitialized_hooks"/>
