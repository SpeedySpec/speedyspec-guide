## Behaviour

`$wp_filters` is a proxy (`SpeedySpec\WpHooks\CountGlobal`) over the store's counts. Reading a count and setting one behave as in WordPress. <Ev scenario="wp_filters_read_and_write"/>

## Porting

To count from a point, subtract an earlier `did_filter()` instead of resetting `$wp_filters`. <Ev break="wp_filters_read_and_write"/>
