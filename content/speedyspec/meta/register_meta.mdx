## Behaviour

Native, in WordPress's order: the legacy callable forms, the `register_meta_args` filter, the item schema check for arrays shown in REST, the revisions check, the `auth_callback` fallback to [is_protected_meta()](/speedyspec/meta/is_protected_meta/), the sanitize and auth filters, then the default's type. It writes the key to `$wp_meta_keys` and hooks [filter_default_metadata()](/speedyspec/meta/filter_default_metadata/) once when a default is given.
