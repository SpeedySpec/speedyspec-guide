## Behaviour

Native: the `DeleteMetadata` command. It deletes the key's rows, or only those holding `$meta_value`, or with `$delete_all` the key on every object. It sends `meta_value` to the `delete_{$meta_type}_metadata` filter only when it isn't `''`, `null` or `false`, clears each touched object's cache entry, and fires `delete_{$meta_type}_meta` and `deleted_{$meta_type}_meta`, plus `delete_postmeta` and `deleted_postmeta` for posts. <Ev scenario="delete-key-post" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-key-thing" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-by-value" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-all" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-all-users" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-legacy-postmeta" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-short-circuit" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-invalid" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="delete-slashed" area="metadata"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-meta-tests::use_cases::delete_all_clears_every_object_cache"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-meta-php::wordpress::wordpress_meta_runs_pass"/>

## Differences

- **`site` on a single site** reads or writes `wp_sitemeta`, where WordPress has no table and returns false; see [_get_meta_table()](/speedyspec/meta/_get_meta_table/). <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-meta-sql/src/repository.rs#L409-L412" hash="29a136921eb9b816414883ee9065616182ae0208fb47091146cd9f6e5179566a"/>
- **Objects** in `$meta_value` reach `delete_{$meta_type}_metadata` as copies, and are matched by their serialized form, as WordPress matches them. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-wp-meta-php/src/codec.rs#L3-L13" hash="9ad5f408eef9d41cda7cf40d1d761ed16a0c161faacd2753523b3e445c4bb0b4"/>
