## Behaviour

Served by WordPress's PHP. The runtime worker links no meta crate, so PHP on SpeedySpec runs WordPress's own `meta.php` and `WP_Meta_Query`. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-php-runtime/Cargo.toml#L20-L55" hash="955f1ccec24cc11da3fa0826db96868561c6e6c3cdc55277b37b80c09b513508"/>
