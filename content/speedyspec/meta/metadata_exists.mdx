## Behaviour

Native: the `MetadataExists` query. A truthy `get_{$meta_type}_metadata` short-circuit answers true; otherwise it reads the cache entry, loading it on a miss. As in WordPress 7.2, a cached entry that isn't an array is a miss. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/meta.php#L823-L824" hash="d1345e17d9a9ea3132f732a9d9a9bd1cf62cb998895e110aa59a35f0ea8c3edf"/> <Ev scenario="get-single-and-multiple-post" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-invalid" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-short-circuit-single" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-cache-miss-exists-and-all" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-reads-cache-entry" area="metadata"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-meta-php::wordpress::wordpress_meta_runs_pass"/>

## Differences
