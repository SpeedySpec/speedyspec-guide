## Behaviour

Native: the `GetMetadataDefault` query. It runs `default_{$meta_type}_metadata` over `''` (single) or an empty array, and wraps an answer that isn't a list for `$single` false. The registered default comes from [filter_default_metadata()](/speedyspec/meta/filter_default_metadata/), hooked on that filter by [register_meta()](/speedyspec/meta/register_meta/). <Ev scenario="get-missing-key" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-registered-default" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-default-filter" area="metadata"/> <Ev scenario="get-default-while-installing" area="metadata"/> <Ev test="speedyspec-wp-meta-php::wordpress::wordpress_meta_runs_pass"/>
