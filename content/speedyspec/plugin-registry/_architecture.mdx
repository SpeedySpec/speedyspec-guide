## The contract and its adapters

- **`speedyspec-hooks`** holds the contract, the `Hooks` trait, and stores nothing. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-hooks/src/contract.rs#L89-L104" hash="fb73297a3763dc1306e13100991127c0b8535caad6a902b87cab57431614e9ee"/>
- **`speedyspec-hooks-memory`** is the in-memory implementation. It passes the contract's conformance suite. <Ev test="speedyspec-hooks-memory::conformance::memory_backend_conforms"/>
- **`speedyspec-php`** declares `SpeedySpec\Hooks\Hook` in PHP over the registry it is given. <Ev test="speedyspec-php::php::case_hook_add_and_run"/>
- The runtime worker gives it a per-request registry, which it replaces with an empty one at the end of each request. <Ev source="ss:crates/speedyspec-php-runtime/src/worker.rs#L190-L195" hash="9198e2cea8a80c537b746fe730e97f75eda1bbae0e415011242834f4d82acb46"/>

## Patterns

- **Ports and Adapters**: the `Hooks` trait, with its memory implementation and the per-request wrapper.
- **Facade**: the PHP class over the registry.

## How to change this

Implement `Hooks` and keep the conformance suite passing; `speedyspec-php`'s checks cover the PHP side.
