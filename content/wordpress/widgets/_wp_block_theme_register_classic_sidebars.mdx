## Behaviour

For a block theme, registers the sidebars a previous classic theme left in the `wp_classic_sidebars` theme mod, so their widgets can be used; hooked to `widgets_init`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/widgets.php#L2163-L2179" hash="9f8073ba42cf0af90f4ac8315fe728b782f911a94ca0cb46e2509fabfb26bf0b"/>
