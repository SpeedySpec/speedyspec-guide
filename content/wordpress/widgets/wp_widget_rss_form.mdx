## Behaviour

Prints the RSS widget's form fields: URL, title, number of items (1 to 20, 10 by default), and whether to show summaries, authors and dates; each field can be hidden or left out. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/widgets.php#L1721-L1788" hash="9d731e77f0b2836a4e0df17d7918307d5585d384bd903f338f647c3a3008c2fc"/>
