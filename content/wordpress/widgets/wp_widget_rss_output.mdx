## Behaviour

Prints a feed's items as a list (1 to 20, 10 by default), with summaries, authors and dates when asked. A feed that fails shows its error only in the admin or to users who manage options; an empty feed says it may be down. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/widgets.php#L1607-L1707" hash="29babfd174eb83f8615c757d26262d7e2446db5439d962c3e35e13b006683c34"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
