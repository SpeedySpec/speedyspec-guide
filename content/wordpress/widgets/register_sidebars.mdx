## Behaviour

Registers several sidebars with the same arguments: with more than one, names come from a `%d` pattern (Sidebar %d by default); IDs come from the ID given, with `-2`, `-3` added on conflicts, or are `sidebar-N`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/widgets.php#L174-L214" hash="627755d2de2c81b1b97258863b0897a81418b81e674a5f71fffdd489dcbb1d75"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
