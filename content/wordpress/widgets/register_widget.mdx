## Behaviour

Registers a widget with the global widget factory: a `WP_Widget` subclass by name, or an instance. Its instances are registered at `widgets_init`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/widgets.php#L120-L124" hash="486ef57ee38f08f1a15b644d84a8ae986dbc6ae628bcf58ba8d13ce511635276"/>
