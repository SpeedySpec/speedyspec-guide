## Behaviour

Converts a single widget's settings to the multi-widget form, as number 2, and renames the widget in the sidebars; in the admin it saves both. Settings already numbered only gain `_multiwidget`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/widgets.php#L1147-L1202" hash="89d6b3994c2e72eaa7a00658f060c5047453ba359f9369b99ee3d4e608ad04d4"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
