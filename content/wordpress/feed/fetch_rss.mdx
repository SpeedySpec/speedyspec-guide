## Behaviour

Fetches and parses an RSS feed with MagpieRSS, cached in transients for an hour; gives the MagpieRSS object or false. Part of MagpieRSS, deprecated since 3.0; use fetch_feed(). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/rss.php#L412-L536" hash="86c4deb8a5b5f2c655d3c79547d2e238fbaee80192d7ea2eeb155c5f55e44193"/>
