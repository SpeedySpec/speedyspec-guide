Builds the requested fields of a post: `id` always, and the other fields the post type supports and the user may see. The `raw` sub-fields are for editors only, and protected content is withheld. A field with no sub-field left is left out. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/abilities/class-wp-abilities-content.php#L1450-L1540" hash="b51791c61b3194ac0cb32f5c8dbc6990c1470b8c7a7f723090788bcde67e82f6"/>
