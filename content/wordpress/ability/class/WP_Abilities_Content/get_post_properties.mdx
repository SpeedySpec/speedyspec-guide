The schemas of the post fields, in output order: `id`, `type`, `status`, `date` and `modified` (`site` and `gmt`), `slug`, `link`, `title`, `excerpt` and `content` (`raw`, `rendered`, and `protected` for the last two), `author` (`id`, `name`, `slug`) and `parent`.
