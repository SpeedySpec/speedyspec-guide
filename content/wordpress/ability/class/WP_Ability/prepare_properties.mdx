Checks and defaults the properties. `label`, `description` and `category` must be strings. For `WP_Ability` itself, `execute_callback` must be callable, and there must be a callable `permission_callback` or a non-empty `required_capabilities` list. Schemas and meta must be arrays, and meta's `show_in_rest` and `public` booleans. Otherwise it throws an `InvalidArgumentException`.
