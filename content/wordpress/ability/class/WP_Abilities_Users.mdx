Registers `core/users-query`, which lists users with chosen fields, one page at a time. It needs a logged-in user. Callers who can't `list_users` see only authors of published posts, and the roles and usernames filters are refused to them. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/abilities/class-wp-abilities-users.php#L36-L1030" hash="5ff034e17ce6de2b96b83765e8a2356102aa74596128d2e55f125f3989c2e22f"/>
