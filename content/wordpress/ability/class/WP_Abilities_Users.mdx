Registers `core/users-query`, which lists users with chosen fields, one page at a time. It needs a logged-in user. Callers who can't `list_users` see only authors of published posts, and the roles and usernames filters are refused to them. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/abilities/class-wp-abilities-users.php#L36-L1021" hash="2952d8a47ac54d293cf38a5808058d5c98433fe8818598caf5ba43c3f313f470"/>
