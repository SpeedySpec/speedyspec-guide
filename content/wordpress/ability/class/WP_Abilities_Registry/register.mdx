Registers an ability. A name must be `namespace/name` in lowercase letters, digits and dashes, and not taken. After `wp_register_ability_args`, the properties must name a registered `category`; `ability_class`, when given, must extend `WP_Ability`. An `InvalidArgumentException` from the ability's constructor becomes a notice.
