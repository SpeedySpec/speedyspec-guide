Registers `core/content-query`, which reads the posts of a post type exposed to abilities with `show_in_abilities`. Posts are filtered by IDs, slugs, status, author and parent, with chosen fields, one page at a time. It needs a logged-in user, and each post is withheld unless the user may read it, as in the REST posts controller. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/abilities/class-wp-abilities-content.php#L36-L1836" hash="0dfde1ff5b6aa5da89b6eeb1e79faf0ebfb69399845ddd125963eaa48c95a46d"/>
