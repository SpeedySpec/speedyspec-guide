Registers `core/content-query`, which reads the posts of a post type exposed to abilities with `show_in_abilities`. Posts are filtered by IDs, slugs, status, author and parent, with chosen fields, one page at a time. It needs a logged-in user, and each post is withheld unless the user may read it, as in the REST posts controller. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/abilities/class-wp-abilities-content.php#L36-L1837" hash="2d5d3c7c5eb1195c64689142d06197ebfbb8fb1801507a7c5859020cdf3148b5"/>
