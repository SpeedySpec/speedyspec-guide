## Behaviour

Trashes a comment, or deletes it with force or when trash is off, with its meta; its children move to its parent. Fires delete_comment and deleted_comment. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/comment.php#L1582-L1667" hash="67511c21714c6d0d2c035f12de38bc5d25d5c953c7449e5a42734ea9a781adea"/>

## Override

`delete_comment`, `deleted_comment`, `wp_set_comment_status` fire.
