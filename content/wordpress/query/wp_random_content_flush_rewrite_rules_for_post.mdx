## Behaviour

Hooked to wp_after_insert_post and after_delete_post: flushes the rewrite rules when a published page (or a post, when posts share the URL) at the random base changes, since it takes precedence over random redirects. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/query.php#L1874-L1932" hash="eb0ef6701991b0544ee772c94c5c92678635c517db1e1107dd237e473333c6b3"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
