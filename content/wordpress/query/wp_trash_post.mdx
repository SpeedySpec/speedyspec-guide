## Behaviour

Moves a post to the trash, remembering its status and time, trashing its comments; deletes it when trash is off. Fires wp_trash_post and trashed_post. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/post.php#L4275-L4347" hash="960052fa141915ccf2eb3da304bcfbde32b9471dc2e0db70b077a57c7bdc800f"/>

## Override

`pre_trash_post` filters it; `wp_trash_post`, `trashed_post` fire.
