Fires a post's status change hooks: transition_post_status, `{$old}_to_{$new}`, and `{$new}_{$post_type}`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/post.php#L6128-L6205" hash="41f775a725fa8c7a4f2effed4257ba174d8088c6e4d923315441d9b9121027ff"/>
