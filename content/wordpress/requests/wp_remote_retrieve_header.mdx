## Behaviour

One header of a response by name, case-insensitively; a repeated header gives an array of its values, and a missing one or a `WP_Error` gives `''`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/http.php#L264-L270" hash="4b41d1dc5c2534f9e7fdb4e8eee0923a006e13b6cf5ed4ce888939f3dc3c37bf"/>
