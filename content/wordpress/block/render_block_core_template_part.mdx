Renders the Template Part block (core/template-part) on the server, from its attributes, inner content and context: edit the different global regions of your site, like the header, footer, sidebar, or create your own. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/blocks/template-part.php#L19-L181" hash="c87fdba753af75ff6584f77731ed275a7c50c1bfb60ca0bac59892c11bdc9a54"/>
