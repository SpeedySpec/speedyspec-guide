## Behaviour

Renders the Time to Read block (core/post-time-to-read) on the server, from its attributes, inner content and context: show minutes required to finish reading the post. Can also show a word count. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/blocks/post-time-to-read.php#L100-L168" hash="90eb92bde300d24c42630666dfce78a1b0fa5c33de30623d6f058783efb023ad"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
