Builds and sends email: it holds the sender, recipients, subject, body and attachments, builds the MIME message, and hands it to the transport chosen, PHP's `mail()`, sendmail, qmail or SMTP. Errors are in `ErrorInfo`, or thrown as `PHPMailer\PHPMailer\Exception` when it is created with exceptions on. It is PHPMailer 7.2.0, bundled with WordPress; `wp_mail()` uses its subclass `WP_PHPMailer`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/PHPMailer/PHPMailer.php#L32-L5619" hash="00ce622024805ac0dfb067666a180b9032931ead3d8d6c13430ea260974ff2ce"/>
