## Behaviour

Emails a new site's admin the welcome text with their login details, unless wpmu_welcome_notification says no. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-functions.php#L1657-L1767" hash="e0a30ddcc46343d1c1d002f6088bf00641efe2cfafe9b5581a82448c94ca412c"/>

## Override
