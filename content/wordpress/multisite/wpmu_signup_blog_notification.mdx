## Behaviour

Emails a site sign-up its activation link; wpmu_signup_blog_notification can stop it, and its subject and text are filtered. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-functions.php#L942-L1062" hash="8cb51144e941ee8abafdc8fe2cf305cc4edf5459c6b25343e6045ec4880ded6f"/>

## Override

`wpmu_signup_blog_notification`, `wpmu_signup_blog_notification_email`, `wpmu_signup_blog_notification_subject` filter it.
