## Behaviour

Undoes a site's setup: drops its tables (wpmu_drop_tables filters them) and deletes its uploads folder; fires wp_uninitialize_site. An error when not initialized. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-site.php#L792-L900" hash="1df10213965c764d7afb364b87a0d3eae441d1934d3096ab302885d4aabeff22"/>

## Override

`wpmu_drop_tables`, `wpmu_delete_blog_upload_dir` filter it.
