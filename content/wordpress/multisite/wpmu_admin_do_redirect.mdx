## Behaviour

Redirects in the network admin to a URL from the request (ref or redirect) or the referer, then ends the request; deprecated in 3.3.0 for wp_redirect(). Here the redirect is stopped first. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-deprecated.php#L275-L308" hash="d629f5ec31ddaa347da541ef06eca3fc36d2e5612e20f05342a748a9936275ef"/>
