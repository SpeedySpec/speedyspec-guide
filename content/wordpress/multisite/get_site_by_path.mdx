The site best matching a domain and path: the longest path match, checking www and non-www domains, segments limited by site_by_path_segments_count; pre_get_site_by_path can answer. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-load.php#L163-L265" hash="67ce50632f79d30914c800b66440b20860fcd306900b0f9f7053b7e69ceaa9e8"/>
