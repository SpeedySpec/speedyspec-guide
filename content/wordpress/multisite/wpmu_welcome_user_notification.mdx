## Behaviour

Emails a new user the welcome text with their login details, unless wpmu_welcome_user_notification says no. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-functions.php#L1898-L1978" hash="cb5b057f4ec81ceee83b23787401dcd3baf41f18fea0c56f259b361bb198c17d"/>

## Override

`wpmu_welcome_user_notification`, `update_welcome_user_email`, `update_welcome_user_subject` filter it.
