## Behaviour

Checks a sign-up's username and email: lowercase letters and digits, 4 to 60 characters, not all digits or reserved, and neither taken nor pending; wpmu_validate_user_signup filters the result. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-functions.php#L469-L603" hash="4bc192a6d59e9e83c69d177ca406010bf93ff30346caa6274352fa52bbbc5579"/>

## Override

`illegal_user_logins`, `wpmu_validate_user_signup` filter it.
