## Behaviour

Emails a user sign-up its activation link; wpmu_signup_user_notification can stop it, and its subject and text are filtered. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-functions.php#L1086-L1177" hash="14efc17d1d9a9fab8c3dfd9e911815de8800ed4d22fa8ead72f981dae920004d"/>

## Override

`wpmu_signup_user_notification`, `wpmu_signup_user_notification_email`, `wpmu_signup_user_notification_subject` filter it.
