## Behaviour

Lets a request for a site through, or stops it: deleted sites (410), inactive sites and archived or spam sites, each with a drop-in page when there is one; super admins always pass, and ms_site_check can answer. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/ms-load.php#L74-L127" hash="2d0f6c0f47f3e44c0ee678bbf82ebb525bbf3e92b0542682e783bb697ef4ab57"/>

## Override
