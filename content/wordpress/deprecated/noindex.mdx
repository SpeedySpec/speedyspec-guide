## Behaviour

Deprecated in 5.7.0; use wp_robots_noindex(). Displays a `noindex` meta tag if required by the blog configuration. Calling it reports the deprecation (deprecated_function_run). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/deprecated.php#L4202-L4209" hash="61f8acad203e96220d3262abfec5491024fa3110064cbb1114b8f0b0ba1afdf9"/>
