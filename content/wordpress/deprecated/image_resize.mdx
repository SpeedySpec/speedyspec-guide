## Behaviour

Deprecated in 3.5.0; use wp_get_image_editor(). Scale down an image to fit a particular size and save a new copy of the image. Calling it reports the deprecation (deprecated_function_run). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/deprecated.php#L3271-L3290" hash="74e16f7c511837e517f08cdf0585b57fad249b855ab41b5b3a7e7b2c33dbd1bf"/>
