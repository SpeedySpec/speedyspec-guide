## Behaviour

Deprecated in 2.9.0; use _x(). Translates $text like translate(), but assumes that the text contains a context after its last vertical bar. Calling it reports the deprecation (deprecated_function_run). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/deprecated.php#L1793-L1796" hash="bbb72e42453b05ab86bf140657186ee9dd04f3f593abef7e375da2109573be67"/>
