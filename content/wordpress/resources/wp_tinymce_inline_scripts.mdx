## Behaviour

Adds the classic editor's TinyMCE settings and translations inline before wp-block-library, for the block editor's Classic block. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/script-loader.php#L518-L652" hash="fed02758b253fabb41deddc32f58c1eaecf7e42f5e631198872bf57516c4c893"/>
