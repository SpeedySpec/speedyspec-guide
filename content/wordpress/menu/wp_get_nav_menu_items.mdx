## Behaviour

A menu's items, published by default, each set up by `wp_setup_nav_menu_item()`, sorted and renumbered 1, 2, ... in `menu_order`. Outside the admin, invalid items are dropped. `false` for a menu that doesn't exist or when the `nav_menu` taxonomy isn't registered. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/nav-menu.php#L708-L770" hash="6342376e1b07a85de78ae5126c22e037ab3ff6b424229177c3fcc7a3af574f62"/>

## Override

`wp_get_nav_menu_items` filters the items.
