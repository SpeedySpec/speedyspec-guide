## Behaviour

Registers theme menu locations, slug to description, merging over those already registered, and turns on the `menus` theme support. A numeric key is reported with `_doing_it_wrong()`, but is still added. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/nav-menu.php#L90-L103" hash="f32b37125042cb2c1d8da88523185063ae9af9a93c2e28eaf6ffce48e1745290"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
