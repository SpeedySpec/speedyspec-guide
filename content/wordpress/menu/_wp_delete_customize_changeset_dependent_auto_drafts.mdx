## Behaviour

When a Customizer changeset is deleted, deletes the auto-draft posts it created for menus and trashes those since made drafts; hooked to `delete_post`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/nav-menu.php#L1174-L1198" hash="cd7b96b20a97159d8be315fb17213f2ef8462aa85471f19cc9d262fac228c29c"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
