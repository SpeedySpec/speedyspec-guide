## Behaviour

The source and destination coordinates and sizes for resizing an image, cropping when asked, or false when no resize is needed; filtered by image_resize_dimensions. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/media.php#L555-L685" hash="238d5724e2ca0d9ab85816543e37761d7611aaa80b9b39d57e35d9b47221714c"/>

## Override

`image_resize_dimensions`, `wp_image_resize_identical_dimensions` filter it.
