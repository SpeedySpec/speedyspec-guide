## Behaviour

Adds width and height to an img tag in content that lacks them, from its attachment, when wp_img_tag_add_width_and_height_attr allows. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/media.php#L2372-L2413" hash="a394112205557e16377c5a89a7b4f52794034bc5334389341487df79cfeaf4ee"/>

## Override

`wp_img_tag_add_width_and_height_attr` filters it.
