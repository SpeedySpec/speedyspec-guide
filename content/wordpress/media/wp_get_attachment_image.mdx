## Behaviour

The img tag of an attachment's image at a size, with alt, srcset, sizes, loading and decoding; filtered by wp_get_attachment_image_attributes and wp_get_attachment_image. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/media.php#L1169-L1317" hash="1d2eaef00f0040a89fdfa91606078b63291ec538cd34205fdd18338c13d3ed85"/>

## Override

`wp_get_attachment_image_context`, `wp_img_tag_add_auto_sizes`, `wp_get_attachment_image_attributes`, `wp_get_attachment_image` filter it.
