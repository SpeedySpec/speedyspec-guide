## Behaviour

Renders the [gallery] shortcode: the images given by ids, or attached to the post, as a grid of linked thumbnails with captions; post_gallery can replace it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/media.php#L2828-L3086" hash="8fcfd71e99c0c5901bfe7e9a390f8ace54db39bf8c0ed8e43499502dbc136af0"/>

## Override

`post_gallery`, `use_default_gallery_style`, `gallery_style` filter it.
