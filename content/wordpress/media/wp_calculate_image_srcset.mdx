## Behaviour

The srcset value for an image source from its metadata: the same-ratio sizes up to max_srcset_image_width, or false; filtered by wp_calculate_image_srcset. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/media.php#L1444-L1646" hash="6353007805f9e88b97dac0f8cd5c40a3371e4a37a1518709d43eb940a946edbf"/>

## Override

`wp_calculate_image_srcset_meta`, `max_srcset_image_width`, `wp_calculate_image_srcset` filter it.
