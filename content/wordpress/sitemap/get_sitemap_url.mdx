## Behaviour

The URL of the sitemap index, or of a page of a provider's sitemap, optionally for a subtype. An unknown provider or subtype gives `false`; a page under 1 is page 1. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/sitemaps.php#L103-L133" hash="e4177b43425acfc8c25bdd2509c51e1ce30ddc71dd44abb441e17eb342494169"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
