## Behaviour

Prints the login page up to the form: the head with the title `("{title}` ‹ `{site}` — WordPress", with Recovery Mode in front during a recovery session), the login styles, `login_enqueue_scripts` and `login_head`; the body with its classes (the action, the color scheme, an interim login, the locale); the logo link; the message; then the errors and notices of a `WP_Error`, errors whose data isn't `message` in an error notice and the rest in an info notice. When the first error's code is one of the shake codes, `wp_shake_js()` is hooked to `login_footer`. It is declared in `wp-login.php`. <Ev source="wp:wp-login.php#L41-L313" hash="85fb66f2c8f0eadb05055753e4f1d8a605c8190775dc7524836d64ea5239cdd7"/>

## Override

`login_title`, `login_headerurl`, `login_headertext`, `login_body_class`, `login_message`, `login_errors`, `login_messages` and `shake_error_codes` filter its parts; `login_enqueue_scripts`, `login_head` and `login_header` fire in it.
