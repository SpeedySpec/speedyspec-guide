## Behaviour

Logs XML-RPC input or output with `error_log()` when the `xmlrpc_logging` global is set. Deprecated in 3.4.0 for `error_log()`. It is declared in `xmlrpc.php`. <Ev source="wp:xmlrpc.php#L101-L106" hash="a44f18818a02dfa1c5d0abaee4b9802ab9286dbb131179c9ac89280063a3bd28"/>
