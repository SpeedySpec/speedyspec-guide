## Behaviour

Prints the site fields of the signup form: the site name, as a subdirectory or a subdomain of the network, with the address it will have for visitors not logged in; the site title; the language, when languages are installed; and whether search engines may index it. A field's error from the `WP_Error` shows above it. It is declared in `wp-signup.php`. <Ev source="wp:wp-signup.php#L128-L255" hash="8560b17b01cde829873e4f5841f06b2809322b496f6ea59e89dc843b55bc1df2"/>

## Override

`signup_blogform` fires at the end with the errors.
