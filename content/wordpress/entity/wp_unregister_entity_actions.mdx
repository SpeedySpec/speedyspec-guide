## Behaviour

Unregisters some of an entity's actions by id, or all of them, during `wp_entity_actions_api_init`, and returns the definitions it removed. It is `WP_Entity_Actions_Registry::unregister()`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/entity-actions.php#L63-L65" hash="e0dcf1a72d1771d30f732d363ebde28d5207c162bfa21649c64e5455cc3f7359"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
