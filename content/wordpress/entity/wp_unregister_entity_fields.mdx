## Behaviour

Unregisters some of an entity's fields by id, or all of them when no ids are given, on `wp_entity_fields_api_init`, and returns the definitions it removed. It is `WP_Entity_Fields_Registry::unregister()`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/entity-fields.php#L94-L96" hash="7685ac443c016f48de98c335c36b85a62b9599eb983df2e33489d84f1ab83c52"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
