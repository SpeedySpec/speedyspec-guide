## Behaviour

Prints the News part of the Events and News widget from the WordPress news and Planet feeds: from the cache, or a loading note while they load after the page. <Ev source="wp:wp-admin/includes/dashboard.php#L1541-L1628" hash="e79dc1334eec10a9a029961a2d7d8d7a63172e0cfe72849bfb82bcc88fadb7dd"/>

## Override

`dashboard_primary_link`, `dashboard_primary_feed`, `dashboard_primary_title`, `dashboard_secondary_link`, `dashboard_secondary_feed`, `dashboard_secondary_title`, `dashboard_secondary_items` filter it.
