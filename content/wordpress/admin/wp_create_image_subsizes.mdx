## Behaviour

Creates an image's sub-sizes, scaling down an image larger than big_image_size_threshold first, and saves its metadata as it goes; gives the metadata. <Ev source="wp:wp-admin/includes/image.php#L240-L413" hash="6523230d8547b078eebc1daa5576555f9a97b274f18b50819abd7782949bd48c"/>

## Override

`big_image_size_threshold`, `intermediate_image_sizes_advanced` filter it.
