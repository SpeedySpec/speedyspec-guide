## Behaviour

Hooked to wp_handle_upload_prefilter on a network: refuses an upload larger than the network's file size limit or the site's free space, unless the network turned the check off; outside the browser uploader and Ajax it stops with the error. <Ev source="wp:wp-admin/includes/ms.php#L18-L53" hash="306d5cfff43ef37b6f95ef6317e1532e667546b12559f89f5fdc1cc2a074096f"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
