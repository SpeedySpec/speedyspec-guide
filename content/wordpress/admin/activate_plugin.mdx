## Behaviour

Activates a plugin: checks it is valid and its requirements, loads it, runs its activation hook and adds it to active_plugins; null or an error. <Ev source="wp:wp-admin/includes/plugin.php#L647-L748" hash="b87b54a5abdf3bc08b47db6297e6db5ac28202d9a694816f671289e4481fd9f3"/>

## Override

`activate_plugin`, `activate_{$plugin}`, `activated_plugin` fire.
