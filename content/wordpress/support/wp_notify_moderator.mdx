## Behaviour

Emails the site admin (and the author, when they can edit the comment) that a comment waits for moderation, when moderation emails are on. Line breaks in the commenter's name, email and URL become spaces, so they can't add email headers. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/pluggable.php#L2028-L2218" hash="788ae09157d1ed89659240a7381d24c27ff26d79ff4cedc1905b9e7ea78b28a9"/>

## Override

`notify_moderator`, `comment_moderation_recipients`, `comment_moderation_headers`, `comment_moderation_text`, `comment_moderation_subject` filter it.
