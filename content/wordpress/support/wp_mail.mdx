## Behaviour

Sends an email through PHPMailer: to, subject, message, headers and attachments, from WordPress `<wordpress@site>` unless the headers or filters say otherwise; pre_wp_mail can answer instead (as here). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/pluggable.php#L195-L675" hash="634c0f6b1d82b213045931eb4d5c74ae12530f56dfc3d29452eda82e996bee31"/>

## Override

`wp_mail`, `pre_wp_mail`, `wp_mail_from`, `wp_mail_from_name`, `wp_mail_content_type`, `wp_mail_charset`, `wp_mail_embed_args` filter it; `wp_mail_failed`, `phpmailer_init`, `wp_mail_succeeded` fire.
