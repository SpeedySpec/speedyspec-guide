## Behaviour

The markup of an admin notice: its type (success, error, warning, info), dismissible, extra classes and attributes, and the message in a paragraph unless told not to. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/functions.php#L9377-L9474" hash="937c93605498542293ed594f440143c63908dce615d4bd7ae28a03c7e05a1c46"/>

## Override

`wp_admin_notice_args`, `wp_admin_notice_markup` filter it.
