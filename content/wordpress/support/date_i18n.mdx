## Behaviour

Formats a timestamp (or now) with month and weekday names translated; the timestamp is taken as local time unless GMT is asked for. date_i18n filters the result. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/functions.php#L183-L227" hash="44e0d6f17f307851d2a5bd9731eaf226699a2a4624892b15d80c654adcaf1121"/>

## Override

`date_i18n` filters it.
