## Behaviour

Empties the trash: posts and comments trashed longer than EMPTY_TRASH_DAYS ago are deleted; trash meta left on items no longer trashed is removed. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/functions.php#L7113-L7153" hash="b277fafbb301800dee68e810d1236503905df8ae074d75bb0f5e032d5b2ce735"/>
