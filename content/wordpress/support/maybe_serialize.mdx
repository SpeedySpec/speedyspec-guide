## Behaviour

Serializes arrays and objects; strings that are already serialized are serialized again, so they come back as given. Other values are left alone. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/functions.php#L651-L666" hash="1966fbc1617c0c256d420dfc4654b199604d8b8b4d6397ff2de29064816f5647"/>
