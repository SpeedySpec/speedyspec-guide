## Behaviour

Emails a post's author about a new comment, when the comment is approved and the author is neither the commenter nor the current user; the recipients, text, subject and headers are filtered. Line breaks in the commenter's name, email and URL become spaces, and Reply-To is set only for a valid email. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/pluggable.php#L1755-L2011" hash="b284cd2da6e41654ea1ab6cf5309f3370a059dece96740d4c744e90a8efd98a0"/>

## Override

`comment_notification_recipients`, `comment_notification_notify_author`, `comment_notification_headers`, `comment_notification_text`, `comment_notification_subject` filter it.
