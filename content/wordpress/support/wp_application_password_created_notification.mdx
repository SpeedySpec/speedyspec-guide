## Behaviour

Emails a user, in their locale, that an application password was made for their account, with its name; send_application_password_created_notification can stop it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/functions.php#L9600-L9733" hash="456ea9d1f71ee510afeb4d471752f8dfeda4c80d98556a31c5df97c6db2d69cd"/>

## Override

`wp_send_application_password_created_email`, `wp_application_password_created_email` filter it.
