## Behaviour

Adds a feed type, served at `/feed/{name}/`, and hooks its callback to `do_feed_{name}`, replacing any callback WordPress has there. Returns the hook's name. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/rewrite.php#L255-L270" hash="7f3b59d3548f45f9485450aee28240d09b97e6ac3eae6b1cc1f77191b14d9089"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
