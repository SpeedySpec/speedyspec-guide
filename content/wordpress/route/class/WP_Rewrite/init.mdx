Resets the extra rules, external rules and endpoints, and reads the permalink structure and random base. It works out the front (the part before the first tag), the root (`index.php/` for `PATHINFO` permalinks), trailing slashes, and whether page rules come first (the first tag is `%postname%`, `%category%`, `%tag%` or `%author%`).
