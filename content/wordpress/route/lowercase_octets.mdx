## Behaviour

The callback `redirect_canonical()` uses to lowercase percent-encoded octets, so the requested and canonical URLs compare equal. It is declared the first time it is needed. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/canonical.php#L799-L801" hash="d806d56cf36df26bf0e4ad7ca062d02301e8269b317edd474ccc436e84c0c4b5"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
