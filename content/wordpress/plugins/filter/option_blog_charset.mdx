## Behaviour

Fired by `get_option()` for the site's charset; WordPress attaches `_wp_specialchars()` and `_canonical_charset()`, which turns UTF-8 spellings into `UTF-8` and the ISO-8859-1 family into `ISO-8859-1`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/default-filters.php#L301-L302" hash="187969a778696221b5b0261bce34cc63841d503360a6991541ec50d0e71921ce"/>
