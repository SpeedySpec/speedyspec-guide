## Behaviour

Fired by `get_term()` (as `get_{$taxonomy}`) for a `post_format` term; WordPress attaches `_post_format_get_term()`, which gives the term its display name, such as Gallery for `post-format-gallery`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/default-filters.php#L617-L617" hash="bf59233b783f98bfe6e3a3adc23bfcbd33c4f9d686b3e9ace9e9a89a67b437f4"/>
