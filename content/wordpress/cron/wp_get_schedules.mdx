## Behaviour

The recurrences events can use, each with an interval in seconds and a display name: `hourly`, `twicedaily`, `daily` and `weekly`, after any that `cron_schedules` adds. The built-in four can't be replaced. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/cron.php#L1145-L1182" hash="c5d2aa6a4c464cdb00c87449fb5adeff18633cf4b7b1b0406ded59fbc4b1f320"/>

## Override
