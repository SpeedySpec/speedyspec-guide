## Behaviour

Replaces the quotes after numbers with primes (′ and ″), for feet, inches and minutes, telling them apart from closing quotes. `wptexturize()` calls it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L320-L375" hash="1bda39ac6e86260747fe3604f3247f57e145babb59e14e7bed76a44b767f47fb"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
