## Behaviour

Makes a user name safe: tags and entities removed and accents stripped. With `$strict`, only letters, digits, spaces and `_ . - @` are kept. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L2152-L2180" hash="351fd52f430fab3100e342f0d33b3db13c016cd18bbe77e40a047e4cfa647dc5"/>
