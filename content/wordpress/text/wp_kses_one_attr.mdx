## Behaviour

Filters one attribute, written as `name="value"`, for an element, by the `post` allowed list; a disallowed attribute or protocol is removed. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/kses.php#L2064-L2126" hash="720846ac48f1b31f3e0268ad32d67ccc7a7ff481f90afbb98646c853cae78c44"/>
