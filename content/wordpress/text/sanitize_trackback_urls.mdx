## Behaviour

Splits the URLs to ping on whitespace, keeps those starting with `http://` or `https://`, cleans them with `sanitize_url()`, and joins them with newlines. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L5898-L5919" hash="ae8f5916456057cca6c7900745129981e358992354bdbba3e600d3ce3d37d3ed"/>

## Override
