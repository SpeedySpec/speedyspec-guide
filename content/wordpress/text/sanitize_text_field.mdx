## Behaviour

Cleans text from a user or the database: checks UTF-8, escapes a lone `<`, strips tags, collapses whitespace and line breaks, trims, and removes percent-encoded octets. An array or object gives `''`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L5725-L5737" hash="15a01c9f94e2799602c665ccdc550cb1508738dab02b5979e58c21daea94b0b5"/>

## Override

`sanitize_text_field` filters the result.
