## Behaviour

The callback that links a full URL in `make_clickable()`. Its match holds the prefix, the URL and any trailing punctuation, which stays outside the link unless a parenthesis is part of the URL. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L3070-L3102" hash="590dcb0369ab254fbae494a218f1e0c24f28c32b5b86cbfab92f955921013fe4"/>
