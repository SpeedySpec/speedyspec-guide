## Behaviour

The callback `convert_smilies()` uses for one smiley: an emoji character when the smiley maps to one, otherwise an `img` of the smiley from `wp-includes/images/smilies/`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L3566-L3597" hash="35c34976b1eb0bd9ba92a4952ebedb5ee9ba9f5a10d8d2a6c856bb8ade059fdb"/>

## Override

`smilies_src` filters the image URL.
