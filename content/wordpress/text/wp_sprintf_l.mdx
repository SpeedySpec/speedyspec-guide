## Behaviour

Formats `%l` as a list: one item as it is, two joined with "and", more with commas and ", and"; text after `%l` is kept. A fragment not starting with `%l` is returned as it is, and an empty list gives `''`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L5476-L5529" hash="bd4bf0f330d27a66d823f3d12fe52df1c91bf47f4343327eef5cad4a0a2389d8"/>

## Override

`wp_sprintf_l` filters the separators.
