## Behaviour

Removes all tags, and the contents of `script` and `style`, then trims. With `$remove_breaks`, runs of whitespace become one space. `null` gives `''`; an array or object gives `''` with a warning. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L5677-L5713" hash="1ebf1a4bb6d2624045facc6ccdbd052307cddeb2fbf646d99703949d53b884d9"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
