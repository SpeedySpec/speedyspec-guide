## Behaviour

With the `use_smilies` option on, replaces text smilies such as `:)` outside tags and code-like elements with emoji or images; otherwise text is returned as it is. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L3612-L3658" hash="d88c59cda9be75c9f544730a8485bf85007f954fac2bd8ccb256700bba24ec51"/>
