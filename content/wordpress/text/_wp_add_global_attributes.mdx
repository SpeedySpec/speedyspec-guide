## Behaviour

Adds the attributes every element may have, among them `aria-*`, `class`, `data-*`, `dir`, `id`, `lang`, `style`, `title` and `role`, to an element's allowed ones. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/kses.php#L4193-L4226" hash="dd4a5b9b4dfcf891f4db7bb51319db1dfbd9ff1aefd251d0d210a60a451cfe49"/>
