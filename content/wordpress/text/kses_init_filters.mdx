## Behaviour

Adds kses to the filters that save titles, content, excerpts and comments. Comments get `wp_filter_kses()`, or `wp_filter_post_kses()` for a user who may post unfiltered HTML. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/kses.php#L3631-L3650" hash="9b949cb0af5a15bafdc4661faa94f9ab919a81082d1703d0f3ec4a04b0e2e3c9"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
