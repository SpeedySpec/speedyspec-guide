## Behaviour

On `wp_mail`, turns emoji in an HTML message into images. The content type comes from the message's `Content-Type` header, `text/plain` by default. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L6291-L6348" hash="19e449c6f53af33de08f5d0476a51a869fdb535e579d0c9991255694bb9b3efe"/>

## Override

`wp_mail_content_type` filters the content type.
