## Behaviour

Legacy parser: runs every tag in content through `wp_kses_split2()`, leaving text between tags. A stray `>` becomes `&gt;`, and a stray `<` swallows the text up to the next `>`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/kses.php#L2358-L2379" hash="91a823c0da0e610b03b04926a49d551cbe958f8166ad221e85e55ac5f5046ed0"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
