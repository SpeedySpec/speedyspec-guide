## Behaviour

Replaces `print_emoji_styles` on `wp_print_styles` (`admin_print_styles` in the admin) with an inline `wp-emoji-styles` stylesheet; when that callback isn't hooked, it does nothing. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L6030-L6054" hash="58da61c8cd4b485393a18c588e2a9e0bacc68e19e9141d08eb30282c982641ab"/>
