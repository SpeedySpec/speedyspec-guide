## Behaviour

A regular expression fragment for a space: whitespace, a UTF-8 no-break space or `&nbsp;`. It is filtered once and kept for the request. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L6011-L6031" hash="d11a658605df051fd6036ac21fdb09a8bd4b5d40bb66737b15dcefce7f699551"/>

## Override

`wp_spaces_regexp` filters the pattern.
