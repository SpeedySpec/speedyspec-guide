## Behaviour

Converts the numeric entities `&#128;` to `&#159;`, which mean Windows-1252 characters, to the Unicode ones, such as `&#150;` to `&#8211;`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L2558-L2599" hash="19b53445d026eb8a7e645af5db9a30befc6be25ee084dd59edb83561d19a6088"/>
