## Behaviour

Replaces accented letters and some ligatures with plain ASCII ones. Some locales have their own rules: in German, `ä`, `ö` and `ü` become `ae`, `oe` and `ue`. The locale is the site's unless given. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L1614-L2021" hash="0f143fb748245b57788cb34e8b07e954dca2269dfda3d7570e014c20de4a75fc"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
