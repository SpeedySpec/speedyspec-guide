## Behaviour

Makes an excerpt from a post's content when no excerpt is given: blocks rendered, shortcodes stripped, cut to `excerpt_length` words (55) with `excerpt_more` ([…]). A given excerpt comes back as it is. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L4071-L4146" hash="46cde377f093bfc93fcd657bdad389e3d95136a98dc56a623d042f727d80cff5"/>

## Override

`excerpt_length`, `excerpt_more` and `wp_trim_excerpt` filter it.
