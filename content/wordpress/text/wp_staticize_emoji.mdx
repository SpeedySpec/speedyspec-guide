## Behaviour

Replaces emoji with `img` elements from the emoji CDN, outside `code`, `pre`, `style`, `script` and `textarea`. ASCII text is returned at once. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L6196-L6281" hash="2ad9c376b9748aa07f0183f0f9949fbe8510dfeedefaae93df36bdffc759757d"/>

## Override

`emoji_url` and `emoji_ext` choose the images.
