## Behaviour

Adds `rel="noopener"` to links with a `target`, so the page they open can't reach this one through `window.opener`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L3445-L3472" hash="f0ddfbf9838443bb9c603571450e5b7d271dffcd3df3e83749abaa2bb6eb8e2a"/>
