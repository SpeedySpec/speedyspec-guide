## Behaviour

Escapes a `<` that no `>` closes, so kses doesn't take the text after it for a tag. Only in kses's legacy operating mode; otherwise the text is returned as it is. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L5326-L5334" hash="31e98b6d163241b117c15adc1b93942609706c1d9853a5947caa166535aff151"/>

## Override
