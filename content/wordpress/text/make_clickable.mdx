## Behaviour

Turns URLs, `www.` and `ftp.` addresses and email addresses in text into links, outside tags and the contents of `a`, `code`, `pre`, `script` and `style`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L3205-L3279" hash="050cbe1626e0d3ba95376e40cc51740a7ea6b96be8b44a3983ecd068da5bb200"/>

## Override

`make_clickable_rel` filters the links' `rel`.
