## Behaviour

Says whether a string is a plausible email address, returning it, or `false`: a local part, `@`, and a domain of at least two parts with valid characters. It doesn't check that the address exists. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L3673-L3756" hash="db88dd445a6417ed7777b8f3b99c4fe27a2af7b9e4c2e96944f78c9682b6e790"/>

## Override

`is_email` filters the result, with the reason it failed.
