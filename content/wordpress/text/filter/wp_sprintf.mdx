## Behaviour

Fired by `wp_sprintf()` with each `%` fragment and its argument; a changed fragment is used instead of `sprintf()`. WordPress hooks `wp_sprintf_l()` to it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L5446-L5446" hash="6673319805bb87fa79e24f55e1fef9c459f58a363aeead91216f2f932e539fef"/>
