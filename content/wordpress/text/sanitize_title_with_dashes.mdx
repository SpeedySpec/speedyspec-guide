## Behaviour

Lowercases, removes tags, entities and punctuation, and joins words with dashes. Non-ASCII characters are percent-encoded, and some, such as non-breaking spaces and dashes, become plain dashes. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/formatting.php#L2288-L2408" hash="8b6ef6b7c7591427995defe09e327809bfb48cd95e9c0e03ab0c7acfcf52aef6"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
