## Behaviour

Says whether one attribute may stay on an element. `data-*` attributes pass when the element allows `data-*`, and value rules such as `maxlen` and `values` are checked. A disallowed attribute is emptied. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/kses.php#L2684-L2769" hash="49efe9aa7d84c301c0d6ef73e55723b3b6d4159d9b2054173971b99419c24980"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
