Writes a page's `li` and link. Its classes are `page_item` and `page-item-ID`, plus `page_item_has_children`, `current_page_item`, `current_page_parent` and `current_page_ancestor` relative to the current page (or `current_page_parent` for the page for posts). A page without a title shows `#ID (no title)`; the title goes through `the_title`. With `show_date`, the page's date or modified date follows. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/class-walker-page.php#L105-L220" hash="69087e8f21d9317b65b1cb684c72bca43750395cec646cda93076bc8a5444db9"/>
