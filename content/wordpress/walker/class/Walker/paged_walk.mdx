symbol: "method:Walker::paged_walk"

Like `walk()`, one page of top-level items at a time, each with its children, and sets `$max_pages`. A page number under 1 or a negative page size means no paging. `reverse_top_level` and `reverse_children` in the first extra argument reverse the order. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/class-wp-walker.php#L303-L421" hash="67661dfa1b288e5bfec89af8f057070a18f60c033cff43d6aed38f56a67ba292"/>
