Renders a flat list as a tree. Depth -1 writes every item flat, 0 every level, and a positive number that many levels; a depth under -1 or an empty list gives `''`. Items without a parent are the top level; when there are none, the items whose parent isn't in the list are. With depth 0, items whose parent is missing are written after the tree. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/class-wp-walker.php#L194-L280" hash="46ba20805c77ee4f2502fef52182377d23a43c3a61a44b1d8fe51432094eda57"/>
