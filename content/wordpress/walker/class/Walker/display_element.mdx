Writes one item with `start_el()`, then, while `$max_depth` allows (0 is every level), its children between `start_lvl()` and `end_lvl()`, then `end_el()`. It sets `$has_children` first, and removes the children it wrote from the list. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/class-wp-walker.php#L133-L174" hash="f13f055eead2090accd64e96d77842ce3a461eff4ee64d7dd2e0c8f209718b7e"/>
