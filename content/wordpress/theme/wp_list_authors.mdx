## Behaviour

Prints (or gives) a list of authors with posts, linked to their archives, with counts, feeds and full names, as a list or plain. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/author-template.php#L466-L614" hash="bccf8c3087b67d539acac907ea15541fec5bfd59cf3fe2be4a81d99d174ec30e"/>
