## Behaviour

Prints (or gives) the links to a post's pages split by the nextpage tag, numbered or next and previous; filtered by wp_link_pages_args and wp_link_pages. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/post-template.php#L965-L1060" hash="c9d10e1f82b7e90e9b2066d0dec070c86fdf4268a871676b10efd548b9d4a257"/>

## Override

`wp_link_pages_args`, `wp_link_pages_link`, `wp_link_pages` filter it.
