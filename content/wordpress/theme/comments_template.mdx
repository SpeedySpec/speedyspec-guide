## Behaviour

Loads the comments template on singular views (the theme's comments.php, else the compatibility one), with the post's comments queried and threaded; filtered by comments_template_query_args and comments_template. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/comment-template.php#L1441-L1655" hash="d0bf7626426eb640c7399a23574ca77ea5d7117a4c7f35cf8b799c057a8b5b82"/>

## Override

`comments_template_top_level_query_args`, `comments_template_query_args`, `comments_array`, `comments_template` filter it.
