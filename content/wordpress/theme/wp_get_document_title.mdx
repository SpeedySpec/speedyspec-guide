## Behaviour

The document title for the page: its title and the site name joined by a separator (the tagline on the home page, page numbers when paged); filtered by pre_get_document_title, document_title_separator, document_title_parts and document_title. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/general-template.php#L1427-L1548" hash="658e4b119b58768c80ce02e8585df693a4e08cca685406c8e125642fcdb52f68"/>

## Override

`pre_get_document_title`, `document_title_separator`, `document_title_parts`, `document_title` filter it.
