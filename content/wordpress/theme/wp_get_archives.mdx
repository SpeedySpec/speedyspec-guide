## Behaviour

Archive links by year, month, week, day, post or alpha, with counts and limits, printed or given; filtered by getarchives_where and getarchives_join. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/general-template.php#L2280-L2512" hash="7ee18a438e832ecc6ab156a60e5446a0e880b567b6689874be8dbf45f3ffa4dc"/>

## Override

`wp_get_archives_args`, `getarchives_where`, `getarchives_join`, `the_title` filter it.
