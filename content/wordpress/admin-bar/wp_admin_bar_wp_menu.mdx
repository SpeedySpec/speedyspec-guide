## Behaviour

Adds the WordPress logo menu. For a user who can read it links to About, with About and Get Involved under it; on multisite they link to the user's dashboard, and otherwise the logo has no link. The wordpress.org links go in `wp-logo-external`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/admin-bar.php#L144-L254" hash="8924d173532b83df4ef5bc300d71b9d0fc619496ec28393538a41ff3de70189a"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
