## Behaviour

Replaces `_admin_bar_bump_cb` on `wp_head` with inline CSS on `admin-bar` that moves the page down for the bar. A theme that gives its own `admin-bar` callback, or a plugin that unhooked it, gets nothing. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/admin-bar.php#L1425-L1450" hash="6def88cc3aee85384c1ba935c79a4123c355b1fd3b780db816bd20cec19d73cc"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
