## Behaviour

Adds the account item in `top-secondary` for a logged-in user: their display name (or login) and avatar, linking to their profile when they can read. Without an avatar it shows the people icon. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/admin-bar.php#L288-L323" hash="5b7e75b48527f073cb51dcc215d2f6cc8180602a1b0461035b571b5ae0d93e4a"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
