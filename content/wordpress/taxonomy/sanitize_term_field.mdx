## Behaviour

Cleans one term field for a context: IDs and counts as integers; raw untouched; edit, db, display and attribute contexts run the {$field} filters (pre_term_{$field} for db, term_{$field} for display). <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/taxonomy.php#L1865-L2021" hash="459800c24c64db6774e287fe614720ee5c05179133f2468ccaab9773dee4288c"/>

## Override

`edit_term_{$field}`, `edit_{$taxonomy}_{$field}`, `pre_term_{$field}`, `pre_{$taxonomy}_{$field}`, `pre_category_nicename`, `term_{$field}_rss`, `{$taxonomy}_{$field}_rss`, `term_{$field}`, `{$taxonomy}_{$field}` filter it.
