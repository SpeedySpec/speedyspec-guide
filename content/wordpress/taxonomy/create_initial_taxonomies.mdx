## Behaviour

Registers WordPress's taxonomies: category, post_tag, nav_menu, link_category, post_format, wp_theme, wp_template_part_area and wp_pattern_category, with their rewrite slugs from the category_base and tag_base options. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/taxonomy.php#L25-L264" hash="8ae4d433260c4b0c4715efbb9dd84aafa3978abe924c7c73b1f58918c8f4a305"/>

## Override

`post_format_rewrite_base` filters it.
