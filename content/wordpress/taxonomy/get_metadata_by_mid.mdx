## Behaviour

A meta row by its meta ID, as an object with the value unserialized, or false. update_metadata_by_mid() and delete_metadata_by_mid() change or remove it by ID. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/meta.php#L862-L917" hash="27712a7140a24b065fa3f35c71a518abb7ea200750c4623e7a90d04018ce61bb"/>

## Override

`get_{$meta_type}_metadata_by_mid` filters it.
