## Behaviour

A slug unique for a term: in hierarchical taxonomies its parents' slugs are added first, then a number; wp_unique_term_slug filters it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/taxonomy.php#L3271-L3351" hash="7618b17edbf66e867573d5b6ad8a4c8df271262cb665fb5ae6c0fbfb08976834"/>

## Override

`wp_unique_term_slug_is_bad_slug`, `wp_unique_term_slug` filter it.
