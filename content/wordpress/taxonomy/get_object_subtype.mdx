## Behaviour

An object's subtype for meta: a post's type, a term's taxonomy, comment and user for those; `get_object_subtype_{$type}` filters it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/meta.php#L1864-L1938" hash="a0b8124be3452a7a0f878164788adb9aaf2cf271b38c5c99fff525431810cf0a"/>
