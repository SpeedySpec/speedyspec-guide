## Behaviour

A term by ID or object (in a taxonomy when given), as a WP_Term, array or with a filter context; get_term and get_{$taxonomy} filter it. A term of a shared ID in another taxonomy, or a missing term, gives null; a missing taxonomy an error. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/taxonomy.php#L998-L1080" hash="388183721e54c3df65453dd48c3ad42ce4d261463104e714641a5e83dd741cb0"/>

## Override

`get_term`, `get_{$taxonomy}` filter it.
