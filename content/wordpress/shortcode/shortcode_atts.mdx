## Behaviour

Combines a shortcode's attributes with defaults. Only names in the defaults are kept, and a missing one gets its default. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/shortcodes.php#L676-L706" hash="ce9ddf6f7055d59f7f1a86b85a775c98bd31c7369ee15a4001124e21198a3173"/>

## Override

With a shortcode name as the third argument, `shortcode_atts_{$shortcode}` filters the result. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/shortcodes.php#L676-L706" hash="ce9ddf6f7055d59f7f1a86b85a775c98bd31c7369ee15a4001124e21198a3173"/>
