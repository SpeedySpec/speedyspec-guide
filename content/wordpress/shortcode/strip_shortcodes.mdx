## Behaviour

Removes the registered shortcodes from content, with their enclosed content, and leaves unregistered tags and escaped `[[tag]]`s as `[tag]`. Shortcodes inside HTML attributes are escaped first and then restored. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/shortcodes.php#L749-L790" hash="37c3c059ce2c87c1c5336d27b100fcf30589ec7171ca2b78cab32810778eff19"/>

## Override

`strip_shortcodes_tagnames` chooses which of the registered tags are removed. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/shortcodes.php#L749-L790" hash="37c3c059ce2c87c1c5336d27b100fcf30589ec7171ca2b78cab32810778eff19"/>
