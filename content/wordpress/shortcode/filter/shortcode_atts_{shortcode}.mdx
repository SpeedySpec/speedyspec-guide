## Behaviour

Fired by `shortcode_atts()` when it is given a shortcode name, with the combined attributes, the defaults, the attributes given and the name. Its result is what `shortcode_atts()` returns. For example, `shortcode_atts_gallery` for `gallery`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/shortcodes.php#L707-L737" hash="ce9ddf6f7055d59f7f1a86b85a775c98bd31c7369ee15a4001124e21198a3173"/>
