Splits a directive attribute's name into its prefix, its suffix after `--`, and its unique id after `---`, for example `data-wp-on--click---a`. A name with other characters, or starting with a dash, gives `null`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/interactivity-api/class-wp-interactivity-api.php#L908-L974" hash="3b228f9eac5dd00215ec46ea8d0bab1aa1195d1ea4aa33ea48772252ebc0bb7d"/>
