## Behaviour

Fired by `wp_filter_default_autoload_value_via_option_size()`, with 150000 and the option name. Its result, as an integer, is the largest serialized value in bytes that is still autoloaded by default. <Ev scenario="size_rule_filtered" area="options"/> <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/option.php#L1364-L1364" hash="230a0de58ee09f5d254216c03cee75eb5f9b984caf149c7aa441fe941b42123d"/>
