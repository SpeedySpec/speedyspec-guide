## Behaviour

Deletes a site transient and its timeout, and returns whether it was deleted. <Ev scenario="site_transient_single_site" area="options"/> <Ev scenario="site_transient_multisite" area="options"/> With a persistent object cache, or while installing, it deletes from the `site-transient` cache group. <Ev scenario="transient_ext_object_cache" area="options"/>

## Override

It fires `delete_site_transient_{$transient}` first, whether or not anything is deleted, and `deleted_site_transient` only after a delete. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/option.php#L2524-L2524" hash="d1188584e0d628401b637f31a976df3ae12e32b375ca316d963b3423b835acb2"/> <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/option.php#L2547-L2547" hash="007fa3675395f9f47c6dd95725f56cc4fd4b34cf315e359df8809ecd00ddb299"/>
