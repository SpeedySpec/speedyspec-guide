## Behaviour

The `default_option_{$option}` callback `register_setting()` adds when a setting has a `default`. It returns that default, unless the caller of `get_option()` passed one of its own, or the option isn't registered. <Ev scenario="filter_default_option_passed" area="options"/>

## Override

Unregistering the setting removes it.
