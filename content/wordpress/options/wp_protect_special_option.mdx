## Behaviour

Stops with `wp_die()` when given `alloptions` or `notoptions`, the names of WordPress's own option cache entries; any other name passes. <Ev scenario="protect_special_option_direct" area="options"/> `add_option()`, `update_option()` and `delete_option()` call it.

## Override

Not overridable; `wp_die_handler` decides how it stops.
