## Behaviour

The embed HTML for a URL from its oEmbed provider, found by known patterns or, when allowed, by discovery; `false` when nothing can embed it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/embed.php#L113-L116" hash="b65b8fd37e7c098485cd275b5fd83d12051260c432134a320fab90479bd1f215"/>
