## Behaviour

Removes an oEmbed provider by its pattern; `true` once removed. Before `plugins_loaded` the removal is queued and it returns `false`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/embed.php#L166-L179" hash="5167a61d812dc1686bdd571fb65c61ce56784329fb17c0008b5bd2413ad24d4b"/>
