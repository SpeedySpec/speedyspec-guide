## Behaviour

Reads a file's contents after resolving its real path, or `false` when it isn't a file. It is declared in the old `wp-tinymce.php` loader, kept for plugins that load TinyMCE through it. <Ev source="wp:js/_enqueues/vendor/tinymce/wp-tinymce.php#L16-L27" hash="936c514f63ea6f1e1505b20583cc4ffb8d73a2cbd34c4edd7a57afbd5bafd42f"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
