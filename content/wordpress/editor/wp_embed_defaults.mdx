## Behaviour

The default size of an embed: the theme's `$content_width` (500 without one) wide, and 1.5 times that high, at most 1000. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/embed.php#L67-L92" hash="d46810da6d2f19197d954fcf6268862210cc377ce060882223ef73071bea2f11"/>

## Override

`embed_defaults` filters the size.
