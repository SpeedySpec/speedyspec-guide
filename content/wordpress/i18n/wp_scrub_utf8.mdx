## Behaviour

Replaces each maximal invalid span of a string with U+FFFD, with mbstring (PHP 8.1.6 and later) or `_wp_scrub_utf8_fallback()`. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/utf8.php#L109-L122" hash="797332817d2d1697d1dc32a93cedd0f3e35f62ea45e299fdb52e680da6efc745"/>

## Override
