## Behaviour

Registers a theme's languages folder (the theme's folder by default) for its text domain and returns `true`; the translations load just in time, `WP_LANG_DIR/themes` first. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/l10n.php#L1188-L1207" hash="b8e4ea615666aa5a94bc8b7268e2079a8eb908014939d70266bf003297a8df93"/>

## Override
