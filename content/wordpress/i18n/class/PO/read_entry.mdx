Reads the next entry from a PO file: comments, `msgctxt`, `msgid`, `msgid_plural` and `msgstr` lines and their continuations. Returns the entry and the line number, `false` at the end, or `false` on a malformed line. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/pomo/po.php#L328-L450" hash="3db571e50bbd612c6644f9514a8646eed35f6078ca04f9d59ca5e7ed3629c142"/>
