## Behaviour

Loads a text domain's translations the first time one of its strings is translated, from the path the registry finds for the determined locale. Domains unloaded on purpose are skipped. Returns whether it loaded. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/l10n.php#L1525-L1568" hash="7e47eb984338af3f9feb0f4bc153a849aaca839fc7deeca5eb80fcf36662c7c1"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
