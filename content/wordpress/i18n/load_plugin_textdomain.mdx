## Behaviour

Registers a plugin's languages folder (the plugins folder, or a path within it) for its text domain and returns `true`. The translations load just in time, `WP_LANG_DIR/plugins` first. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/l10n.php#L1108-L1132" hash="a2a29cb2e3ee1c0d43b5aec9cb71aac7936cf66f4d9c6c934c035eec1e6465a6"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
