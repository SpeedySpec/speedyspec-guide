## Behaviour

PHP's `clamp()`: the value limited to the minimum and maximum. A NAN bound, or a maximum under the minimum, throws a `ValueError`. WordPress defines it only when PHP doesn't have it. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/compat.php#L715-L751" hash="0248f60c271179a8891f46149ad7e235ce2acb2f6cb0e82448f88fdff7a3e605"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
