## Behaviour

A string's length in characters when the encoding (or else `blog_charset`) is UTF-8, in bytes otherwise; an invalid span counts as one character. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/compat.php#L375-L379" hash="da1aef638a9b7774effb20e63fe54ae33e4a4b329718c97dc2328b22a4c23925"/>
