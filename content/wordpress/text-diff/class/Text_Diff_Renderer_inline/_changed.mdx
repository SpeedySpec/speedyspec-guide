Prints a change. At the `lines` level it diffs the two sides again, word by word, or character by character with `split_characters`, and prints that diff with a renderer one level down. At the `words` and `characters` levels it prints the deletion and then the addition, keeping leading spaces outside the marks. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/Text/Diff/Renderer/inline.php#L132-L179" hash="284c0da7ec6d751d89e8f0d2a970897afabb98fd9663b82aeae56172d6fcf905"/>
