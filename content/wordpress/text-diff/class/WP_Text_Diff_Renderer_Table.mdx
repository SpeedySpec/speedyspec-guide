The renderer behind `wp_text_diff()` and the revisions screen: it prints table rows, two cells per row in the split view or one in the single column view. Each cell has a screen reader label: Added, Deleted or Unchanged. Changed lines are paired with the most similar line on the other side and marked word by word, unless they are too different. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/class-wp-text-diff-renderer-table.php#L21-L628" hash="5e360cad2b0ea833d39ef7173c4332ff114f2748f5f0df6f5b88508ed9e62198"/>
