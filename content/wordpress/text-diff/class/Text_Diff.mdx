Compares two lists of lines and keeps the result as a list of edits (`Text_Diff_Op` objects): copies, deletions, additions and changes. It is the PEAR Text_Diff package, bundled with WordPress. Its engine computes the edits, and a `Text_Diff_Renderer` prints them. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/Text/Diff.php#L18-L265" hash="e220f2d4e76be0fa4b5f71e083cf4d649ec64540d01e74505c3b090094876de3"/>
