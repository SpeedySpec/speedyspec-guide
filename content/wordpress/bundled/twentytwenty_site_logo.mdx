## Behaviour

Prints (or gives) the site logo, or the site title linked home when there is none, in an h1 on the front page and posts page and a div elsewhere; the arguments and the markup are filtered. <Ev source="wp:wp-content/themes/twentytwenty/inc/template-tags.php#L34-L102" hash="1480b2b37c0db6f615349841e28cacbda3227ef25a868ae2d74ba802e7117bf8"/>

## Override

`twentytwenty_site_logo_args`, `twentytwenty_site_logo` filter it.
