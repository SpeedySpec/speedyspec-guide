## Behaviour

Prints a post's footer meta: in lists, Featured for sticky posts, the date, the edit link and the categories and tags; on single posts, the date, author and edit link, then the categories and tags. Nothing for other post types. <Ev source="wp:wp-content/themes/twentytwentyone/inc/template-tags.php#L66-L165" hash="d6b4919c61e1538b6396a1c4afc8f1a53ebe7b01f52265001e77a88bd51e0415"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
