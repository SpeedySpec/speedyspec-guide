## Behaviour

Adds body classes: singular, the cover and full width templates, enable-search-modal, has- or missing-post-thumbnail, pagination, comments, avatars, footer-top-visible or -hidden, and reduced-spacing when the header and background colors match; on body_class. <Ev source="wp:wp-content/themes/twentytwenty/inc/template-tags.php#L688-L774" hash="87d1199c684fdc62ecd17447ef10cf87efe5c5f45d8b621e3fd0e5aae4678541"/>

## Override

Not overridable.
