## Behaviour

Downloads a URL to a temporary file and gives its path, or an error; checks the signature when asked. <Ev source="wp:wp-admin/includes/file.php#L1157-L1357" hash="d0a8ca95b22a09c7d506abd9cc7a453347d2a1219f4b5e8146f56a1a196c7fb4"/>
