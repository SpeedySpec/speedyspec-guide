## Behaviour

Asks WordPress.org for core updates, sending the site's versions and statistics, and stores the answer in the update_core transient, at most twice a day unless forced. <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/update.php#L31-L339" hash="9ca9c3bc7205a876f91790fe66cbe964e1c806e99c507b283e2f039f64d3024e"/>

## Override

`core_version_check_locale`, `core_version_check_query_args` filter it; `wp_maybe_auto_update` fires.
