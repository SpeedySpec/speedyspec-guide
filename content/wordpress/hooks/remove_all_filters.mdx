## Behaviour

Removes every callback on a hook, or only those at one priority, and returns `true`. When none are left, it removes the hook from `$wp_filter`. <Ev scenario="remove_all_filters"/> <Ev source="wp:wp-includes/plugin.php#L354-L362" hash="73280cc67b4f35aa8db33b555a47d48dc728b9249b1d0b759e74abe6f34027bb"/>
